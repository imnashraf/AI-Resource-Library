Setting Up

  1. Go to claude.ai and create an account with your email address.
  2. Choose your plan. Pro is the recommended starting point.

02: The Basics

Before diving into advanced workflows, you need to understand how to actually talk to Claude well. The quality of what you get back is almost entirely determined by the quality of what you put in.

How to Prompt Properly

Most people write to Claude the same way they would type a Google search. Short. Vague. No context. The output reflects that.

Claude works much better when you treat it like a briefing to a very capable person who knows nothing about your situation. Give it context, give it the format you want, and tell it what success looks like.

The four parts of a good prompt:

  • Role: who Claude should be in this response
  • Context: what is happening and why
  • Task: what you actually want it to do
  • Format: how you want the output structured

Weak prompt:

Write me a cold email.

No context. No audience. No tone. Claude will produce something generic.

Strong prompt:

You are a direct response copywriter. I run an AI automation agency called Briix targeting small business owners in the UK. Write me a cold email to a restaurant owner who has not adopted any AI tools yet. The email should be 150 words max, conversational tone, one clear CTA to book a 20-minute call. No jargon.

Specific role, specific target, specific constraints, specific outcome. Claude produces something you can actually send.

Uploading Files and Documents

Claude can read and work with files you upload. This changes how you interact with it for any document-heavy task.

  • PDFs: reports, contracts, research papers, books
  • Word documents: briefs, proposals, scripts
  • Spreadsheets: data, lists, client records
  • Images: screenshots, designs, photos
  • Code files: debugging, refactoring, understanding existing code

Example from Max: Upload a 60-page client report and ask Claude to summarise the three most important findings and suggest next steps in bullet points. Takes 30 seconds instead of an hour.

Using Web Search

Claude has access to web search on Pro and above. This means it can pull current information rather than relying only on its training data.

  • Current news and events
  • Real-time pricing and market data
  • Recent product launches and updates
  • Any topic where the information changes frequently
Search the web for the latest Claude model updates in 2026 and summarise what has changed in the last 3 months.

Claude searches, reads the results, and gives you a concise answer rather than a list of links.

03: Everyday Uses

These are the tasks that save hours every week once you build them into your workflow.

Writing and Content

Claude is one of the best writing tools available. Not because it writes for you, but because it makes your writing faster and sharper when used correctly.

Email sequences

Write me a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers to my email list. My audience is small business owners interested in AI and automation. Tone is conversational and direct. British English. Each email should be under 200 words. Include a clear one-line subject for each.

Specific audience, specific tone, specific format constraints. Output is usable immediately.

Landing pages and copy

Write landing page copy for a service that helps UK restaurant owners automate their booking and customer follow-up using AI. Audience: restaurant owners aged 35 to 55 who are not tech-savvy. Tone: clear and benefit-focused. Structure: headline, subheadline, 3 benefit sections, social proof placeholder, CTA.

The structure instruction means Claude produces something with real shape, not just a block of text.

SEO content

Write a 600-word blog post targeting the keyword "AI tools for small businesses UK". Write for a business owner who is curious but not technical. Include the keyword naturally 3 to 4 times. No jargon. Conversational tone. End with a CTA to book a free consultation.

Telling Claude the keyword count and tone prevents the robotic over-optimised output that most AI content produces.

Research and Analysis

Claude with web search turned on is one of the fastest research tools available.

Competitor analysis

Search the web and find the top 5 AI automation agencies in the UK. For each one, tell me: what they offer, who their target market is, what their pricing looks like if visible, and one thing they do well. Present it as a comparison table.

Claude searches, reads the sites, and structures the output into something you can actually use.

Summarising long documents

I am uploading a 40-page market research report. Read it and tell me: the three most important findings, any risks or opportunities mentioned, and the single most actionable recommendation for a small AI agency. Keep it to one page.

The constraint "keep it to one page" stops Claude from producing an equally long summary.

Documents and Files

04: Projects and Memory

This is where Claude becomes genuinely powerful for ongoing work. Projects let you give Claude persistent context so it does not start from zero every time you open a new conversation.

What Projects Actually Do

A Project is a folder that holds:

  • A custom instruction that Claude reads before every conversation
  • Files and documents you upload as reference material
  • A shared conversation history within the project

The instruction is the most important part. Think of it as the brief that never expires. Claude reads it at the start of every session inside that project.

Example Project instructions:

Client project: "You are working with Briix, an AI automation agency based in Manchester. Our client is a UK restaurant chain with 8 locations. Their goal is to reduce admin time by 50% using AI. When helping with this project, always consider the client's limited tech literacy and their budget constraints. Always suggest practical, low-risk implementations first."
Content project: "You are helping Max Johnson create TikTok scripts. Max is a British founder building an AI agency. His tone is direct and understated. No hype. No exclamation marks. Every sentence must be load-bearing. The CTA format is always: Comment [WORD] and I'll send you [specific thing]."

The Branding Skill

Example from Max: Max showed how to upload his brand assets to a Project and then ask Claude to create a skill that applies that branding to every document it produces. Once built and saved to the skills folder, every document Claude creates from that point forward comes out in the right branding automatically.

To replicate this:

  1. Create a Project and upload your brand guidelines, logo files, and a colour palette document.
  2. Add an instruction that tells Claude to always apply these assets to any document it creates.
  3. Ask Claude to build a skill from this that you can reuse across all your Claude Code projects.
  4. Add the skill to your skills folder. From that point, every document is on-brand.

05: Skills and Tools

Claude Skills are modular add-ons that give Claude specific expertise or capabilities it does not have by default. There are over 60,000 available.

What Skills Actually Are

A skill is a SKILL.md file that gives Claude a structured methodology for a specific type of task. When you install a skill, you are giving Claude a playbook it can call on whenever it detects a relevant task. Skills install with a single command and activate automatically.

Note: Skills require Claude Code to install. Once Claude Code is running, installing any skill takes under 30 seconds.

The Essential Skills

1. marketingskills by Corey Haines:20+ marketing skills in one install. SEO, copywriting, email sequences, CRO, competitor analysis, and growth engineering.

npx skills add coreyhaines31/marketingskills

Example from Max: After installing this, Max asked Claude to write a full email sequence and build a functioning landing page for Briix. Both came back polished and ready to use.

2. UI-UX-Pro-Max: Gives Claude real design intelligence. Understands visual hierarchy, colour psychology, and design systems. Stops Claude producing the generic AI-generated look.

npx skills add nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill@ui-ux-pro-max

3. superpowers (120k GitHub stars): A complete software development methodology. TDD built in, structured debugging, plan-before-build workflow.

npx skills add obra/superpowers

4. Official Anthropic File Skills: Three official skills from Anthropic that let Claude create and edit your actual files directly.

npx skills add anthropics/skills@pdf npx skills add anthropics/skills@pptx npx skills add anthropics/skills@docx

5. find-skills (418k installs): The meta-skill. Describe what you want to do and Claude searches the skills marketplace and recommends the best options to install. Install this first.

npx skills add vercel-labs/find-skills

06: Claude Code

Claude Code is a terminal-based agent that can write code, run it, read your file system, manage projects, and work autonomously on complex multi-step tasks. It is the most powerful way to use Claude.

What Claude Code Can Do

  • Write, test, debug, and run code across your entire project
  • Read and edit any file on your system
  • Run terminal commands
  • Build full applications from a brief
  • Work autonomously on tasks while you do something else
  • Connect to external tools via MCP servers

Note: Claude Code requires Node.js version 18 or above. Run "node --version" in your terminal to check.

Setting Up Claude Code

  1. Open your terminal.
  2. Install Claude Code globally.
npm install -g @anthropic-ai/claude-code
  1. Navigate to a project folder and run Claude Code.
cd your-project claude
  1. Claude Code will ask you to log in with your Claude account. Do that and you are ready.

The 10x Faster Workflow

Example from Max: Max covered three things that make Claude Code dramatically faster. Remote control via /rc lets you continue building from your phone. Hooks replace the CLAUDE.md suggestion system with deterministic rules that always run. App previews let Claude look at what it has built and fix its own errors visually rather than reading logs.

Remote control: Type this in your Claude CLI to get a secure URL you can open on your phone and keep building from anywhere.

/rc

Hooks: Hooks are deterministic rules that run automatically after every code change. Unlike CLAUDE.md which Claude can ignore, hooks always fire.

Set up a hook that automatically runs my test suite and a code quality check after every file change.

Once set, you never have to ask for tests again. They run every time.

Connecting Claude Code to External Tools

MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers are how Claude Code connects to external services. Once connected, Claude can take actions in those tools directly.

Connecting Figma:

claude mcp add figma -- npx @figma/mcp

Other useful MCP connections:

  • PostHog: analytics and A/B testing directly from Claude Code
  • NotebookLM: connect Google's research tool to Claude Code
  • Ruflo: 60-agent multi-agent swarm with self-learning memory

09: Prompt Reference Library

Ready-to-use prompts for the most common tasks. Copy, adapt, and use them.

Writing

Repurpose content:

Take this [blog post / transcript / document] and repurpose it into: 5 LinkedIn post ideas, 3 TikTok hook concepts, and one email newsletter section. Keep the core ideas but adapt the format and length for each platform.

Improve existing writing:

Rewrite the following text to be shorter and more direct. Remove any filler sentences. Every sentence should carry new information. Keep the same meaning but tighten the language. [paste text]

Business

SWOT analysis:

Run a SWOT analysis on my business. Here is the context: [describe your business, market, and current situation]. Be specific and honest. Do not give me generic observations. Focus on things that are actionable.

Pricing strategy:

I run [describe business]. My current pricing is [describe]. My main competitors charge [describe competitor pricing]. Suggest a pricing structure that better reflects the value I deliver and is competitive in my market. Justify each recommendation.

Research

Market research:

Search the web and research the [industry] market in the UK in 2026. Give me: market size, main players, key trends, and one underserved opportunity you can identify. Present it clearly with sources.

Person research:

Research [person name] and tell me: their professional background, what they are currently working on, their public views on [relevant topic], and three things I could reference in a message to them to show I have done my homework.
