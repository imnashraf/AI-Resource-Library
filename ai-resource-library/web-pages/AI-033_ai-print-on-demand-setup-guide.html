Step 01, Pick Your Niche

That's the only real decision you make upfront. Everything else is execution. Pick something specific to a group of people.

Example niches: Gym & Fitness, Travel, Dark Humour, Dog Lovers, Gaming, Mental Health.

TIP: The more specific, the better. "Gym memes" beats "fitness". "Van life travel" beats "travel".

Step 02, Find Winning Designs on Etsy

Before you design anything, search your niche on Etsy. Sort by bestsellers. Look at what is actually selling, the styles, phrases, and product types. These become your design references.

  1. Search your niche, e.g. "gym motivation t-shirt" or "funny travel mug"
  2. Sort by bestseller, Top-left dropdown → Bestsellers
  3. Note repeating themes, Phrases, illustration styles, and colour palettes

Open Etsy: etsy.com

Step 03, Generate Your Design with Claude AI

Open Claude Design and describe what you want. Make sure it resonates with your niche. It generates in about 60 seconds.

Example prompt:

A bold t-shirt graphic for gym lovers. Dark background, teal accent. Motivational phrase: No Days Off. Clean, modern streetwear style.

Open Claude Design: claude.ai/design

Step 04, Set Up Printify & Connect Your Etsy Store

Open a free Printify account, upload your design, drop it onto a product, and connect Printify directly to your Etsy shop.

  1. Create a free Printify account, No upfront cost, you only pay when you make a sale
  2. Choose your product, T-shirts, mugs, hoodies, tote bags, hundreds of options
  3. Upload and position your design, Drop it in, adjust size, placement, and colours
  4. Connect to your Etsy shop, Settings → Sales Channels → Connect Etsy (2 minutes)
  5. Publish the listing, Set your price and go live

Sign up for Printify: printify.com

Step 05, Printify Handles Everything Else

From this point you never touch the product. Someone buys it, Printify prints, packs, and ships it straight to the customer.

Customer Buys → Order placed in your Etsy store automatically.
Printify Prints → Your design sent to nearest print facility.
Ships Direct → Delivered to customer. You keep the profit.
