Indeed + Claude Job Hunter Setup Guide

Max Johnson@maxjohnscn

Indeed + Claude: Your Personal Job Hunter. Connect Indeed to Claude and let it find, filter, and tailor applications for you.

By the end of this guide, Claude will be pulling live job listings from Indeed, matching them to your actual experience, and rewriting your CV for every role you want to apply for. No job board scrolling. No generic applications.

What you'll be able to do:

  • Find the highest-paying roles matched to your skills automatically
  • Filter live Indeed listings without opening a single job board
  • Get your CV rewritten around the right keywords for each specific role
  • Go into every application looking like the strongest possible candidate

What you need:

  • A Claude.ai account (free or Pro)
  • Your CV saved as a PDF or Word document
  • An Indeed account (free to create at indeed.com)

NOTE: The Indeed connector pulls live listings in real time. You'll see roles posted today, not cached results from days ago.

Step 1, Connect Indeed to Claude

Open Claude and go to Settings. Find the Connectors section and look for Indeed. Click Connect and follow the authorisation steps to link your Indeed account.

TIP: The connector only needs read access to Indeed's job listings. It doesn't post anything on your behalf or access your Indeed profile.

Step 2, Upload your CV

In a new Claude conversation, use the attachment button to upload your CV. PDF and Word files both work. Claude will read your experience, skills, and history from the document.

TIP: The more detail your CV includes, the better Claude's job matching will be. If your CV is outdated, update it before uploading.

Step 3, Ask Claude to find the best roles

Once your CV is uploaded and Indeed is connected, paste this prompt into Claude:

I've uploaded my CV. Using the Indeed connector, search for the highest paying jobs that match my experience and skills. Show me the top 10 results with job title, company, salary, and a brief note on why each one is a strong match for my background.

Claude will pull live listings from Indeed and cross-reference them against your actual CV. You'll get a ranked shortlist of real roles, with reasons why each one fits.

Step 4, Filter and refine your results

Not every result will be right. Tell Claude what to filter out and it will re-run the search with tighter criteria. Some examples:

Filter out anything below £50,000. Remote only. No roles requiring more than 3 years in a specific technology I don't have.

TIP: Be specific about location, salary floor, and any deal-breakers. The more constraints you give Claude, the more relevant the shortlist becomes.

Step 5, Tailor your CV for a specific role

Once you've found a role you want to apply for, ask Claude to rewrite your CV around it. Paste the job listing or the job URL and use this prompt:

Using my CV and this job listing, rewrite my CV to be as strong as possible for this specific role. Match the keywords from the job description, lead with the most relevant experience, and reframe any transferable skills to fit what they're asking for. Keep it truthful.

Claude will restructure and rewrite your CV around the exact language and requirements of that role. You're not fabricating anything. You're just presenting what you already have in the way that specific employer is looking for.

IMPORTANT: Always review the tailored CV before submitting. Claude rewrites around keywords and framing, but you know your own experience best. Check every line is accurate.

Step 6, Repeat for every role you want to apply for

Each application gets its own tailored CV. Keep your original CV uploaded in the conversation so Claude always has the full picture to work from.

Prompts to Save

Finding roles:

I've uploaded my CV. Search Indeed for the highest paying roles that match my skills and experience. Focus on [your industry or job type]. Show me the top 10 with salary, company, and a reason why each one fits.

Filtering results:

Remove anything below [salary]. I want roles that are [remote / hybrid / in [city]]. Exclude roles requiring [specific thing you don't have].

Tailoring your CV:

Using my CV and the job listing below, rewrite my CV for this specific role. Use the same keywords as the job description. Lead with my most relevant experience. Keep it accurate. [Paste job listing here]

Writing a cover letter:

Using my tailored CV and this job listing, write a cover letter in a confident, direct tone. First person. No generic opening lines. Make it specific to this company and role.

Tips:

  • Keep your original CV in the conversation, Don't close the chat between tailoring different roles. Claude keeps your original CV in context for the whole session.
  • Search by salary, not just job title, Try prompts like 'find all roles paying over £60,000 that match my experience' rather than searching for a specific title.
  • Claude Pro handles longer CVs and more complex job listings without losing context. If you're applying for senior roles with detailed requirements, Pro will give you better tailoring.
