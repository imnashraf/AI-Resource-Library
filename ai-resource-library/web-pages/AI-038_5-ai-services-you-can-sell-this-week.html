Before We Start, You Don't Need to Know How to Code

The Signal
"I haven't written a single line of code in over 8 months."
Zack Witten, a member of Anthropic's team (the company that builds Claude) stated this publicly. Instead of writing code, he uses Claude to handle everything. When the people who build these tools aren't writing code themselves, the signal is clear. The game has changed at every level.
What Actually Matters Now

It's not syntax. It's judgment: knowing what a good output looks like, understanding what a client actually needs, and being able to direct AI the way a good creative director directs a team. That's a human skill. And it's the valuable one.

The five services in this guide require zero code. They require taste, direction, and the willingness to get started.

Service 01, AI-Powered Copywriting

Businesses need words everywhere: websites, product pages, email campaigns, social ads, sales decks. Most don't have a dedicated writer and are producing mediocre copy because of it. You understand the brand voice, the target customer, and the goal of each piece. AI handles the drafts. Your judgment makes it work.

How to get started

1, Learn the fundamentals of copy first
Before anything else, understand what makes copy convert. Read the free resources at copyhackers.com, specifically the "Copy School" free training. This is what separates you from someone who just prompts AI and pastes the output.

2, Pick your primary AI tools
Start with Claude or ChatGPT for drafting, then add a specialist tool based on your niche. jasper.ai excels at long-form content. copy.ai is strong on sales copy and ad formats. Both have free tiers to test with.

3, Build a portfolio with 3 spec pieces
Pick a niche (SaaS, e-commerce, hospitality) and write three spec pieces as if you already had those clients. A landing page, an email sequence, and a social ad set. This is your proof of concept before your first real client.

TIP

Position yourself as a "brand voice specialist" rather than a generic copywriter. Clients pay more for someone who learns their tone and maintains it across every touchpoint, that's a strategic role, not a commodity.

Service 02, AI Content Repurposing

Businesses create content, podcasts, webinars, long videos, blog posts, and let it die after one use. A 45-minute podcast becomes a blog post, five LinkedIn posts, a thread, three short video scripts, and an email newsletter. AI makes this process fast. You make it coherent and on-brand. This is a recurring service, which means monthly retainers.

The core tool stack

Opus Clip
opus.pro
Automatically clips long video into short-form highlights. Best for turning podcasts and webinars into social-ready clips.
Castmagic
castmagic.io
~£19/mo
Transcribes and extracts structured content from audio and video. The first step in every repurposing workflow.
Repurpose.io
repurpose.io
~£27/mo
Distributes content automatically across platforms. Connects your workflow end-to-end once content is ready.

How to get started

1, Start with one client's existing content library
Find a business with a backlog of podcasts, webinars, or YouTube videos they've never repurposed. Offer to turn their last 3 episodes into a full content set. Charge £300-500 for the test batch.

2, Build a repeatable workflow using Castmagic + Claude
Upload audio/video to Castmagic for transcript and raw output. Then use Claude to refine, reformat, and match brand voice. This two-step process takes under 30 minutes per episode once you've done it a few times.

3, Sell it as a monthly retainer from day one
Price at £500-1,200/month depending on volume. One satisfied client referral in this space typically brings two more, content managers talk to each other.

Service 03, Chatbot Setup & Optimisation

Businesses want chatbots on their website, WhatsApp, and customer service portals. Most owners don't know how to set one up. You set it up, train it on the client's information, test it, and hand it over, then charge for monthly maintenance. This is the highest-perceived-value service on this list because it looks technical. The execution, once you've learned the tools, is entirely manageable.

How to get started

1, Build your first bot in under 2 hours
Sign up for Botpress free, create a new project, and follow the official getting started guide at botpress.com/blog/how-to-build-your-own-ai-chatbot. Build a customer FAQ bot using a fictional business as your practice client.

2, Offer a free setup to your first client
Find a local service business, restaurant, clinic, agency. Offer to build and install their chatbot free in exchange for a case study and testimonial. Then charge £150-200/month for ongoing maintenance and updates.

Pricing guide: Setup fee: £500-1,500 depending on complexity. Monthly maintenance retainer: £150-400. The recurring revenue is the real business, setup is just the door opener.

Service 04, AI Email Marketing + Outbound Systems

Email has the highest ROI of any marketing channel. Most business owners don't have time to do it properly. Combine done-for-you email sequences with a built outbound pipeline and you become a one-stop growth engine for clients, not just a writer.

The outbound stack

Apollo.io
apollo.io
Free to £49/mo
Your starting point. B2B database, contact enrichment, and built-in email sequencing.
Instantly
instantly.ai
~£30/mo
Your sending engine. Handles deliverability, inbox warming, and sending limits at scale.
Clay
clay.com
Scale up to this
For advanced enrichment. Pulls from 150+ data sources to build the richest possible lead lists.

Start with Apollo only. Add Instantly when ready to scale. Move to Clay when volume demands it.

How to get started

1, Start with Apollo only, master it before stacking tools
Apollo's free plan gives 1,200 credits/year. Build a prospect list in a specific niche, write 3 email variants using Claude, and launch a small test sequence. Learn deliverability basics before adding Instantly or Clay.

2, Add Instantly when you're ready to scale sending
Once you understand open rates and reply rates, pair Apollo with Instantly for dedicated sending infrastructure. This combination is what most professional outbound agencies run. Warm new sending domains for 2-3 weeks before going live.

3, Sell it as a done-for-you growth system
Position this as "we build and run your entire outbound pipeline", not just "email marketing." Retainers for this service run £800-2,500/month depending on volume and whether you include lead research.

Service 05, Web Design via Vibe Coding

You don't build websites anymore, you describe them. Tools like Lovable, Bolt, and v0 mean someone with zero development background can deliver a genuinely impressive site in an afternoon. The client perception vs actual effort gap is enormous. Strong margins, fast delivery, easy to demonstrate in a short video.

Which tool to use

Recommended
Lovable
lovable.dev
Most beginner-friendly. Full-stack apps with database and auth from plain English. 5 free credits/day.
Bolt.new
bolt.new
£10/mo paid
Most generous free tier, 1M tokens/month. Full-stack flexibility with clean code export.
v0 by Vercel
v0.dev
Best for polished UI components. Requires more technical context to turn into full sites.

How to get started

1, Build 3 demo sites before approaching any client
Pick three different business types, a restaurant, a consultant, and a local service business. Build each one using Bolt or Lovable in under 2 hours. Screenshot the results. These become your portfolio.

2, Learn to prompt for design, not just structure
The difference between a mediocre vibe-coded site and a great one is in how you describe it. Be specific about mood, style, and layout: "dark, minimal, luxury feel like a premium skincare brand" beats "make it look good."

3, Charge for taste, direction, and speed, not code
A basic 5-page business site should be priced at £500-1,500. A full landing page with custom design: £800-2,500. The client pays for the finished product, not the hours. Your edge is that you can deliver in a day what used to take a week.

Pick One. Start This Week.

You don't need to master all five. Pick the one that matches your existing strengths, learn the tools properly, and land your first client. The market for AI-assisted services is real, the demand is growing, and most businesses are still being badly served. That gap is your opportunity.
