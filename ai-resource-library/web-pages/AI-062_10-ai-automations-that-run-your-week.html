Before You Start

The Two Engines

Every automation in this guide is the same shape: a saved prompt plus a cadence. You write it once, the platform runs it forever. Here is where that lives on each tool.

Claude Cowork scheduled tasks

Click Scheduled in the left sidebar, then New task. Choose Setup manually and fill in the name, the prompt, the approval mode, the cadence and the model. Or choose Create with Claude and describe what you want in plain English.

Cadence options are hourly, daily, weekly, weekdays or manual. Tasks run remotely, so they fire even when your laptop is shut. Anything that needs local files runs locally instead.

ChatGPT Scheduled tasks

Open Scheduled in the sidebar on web, mobile or desktop, then create a new task and pick when it runs. You can also just ask in a chat: describe the job and the timing and it will set the task up for you.

Tasks run at most once an hour. Active task limits are three on Go, five on Plus, ten on Business and fifteen on Pro and Enterprise. Voice chats and custom GPTs are not supported inside a task.

Switch These On First

In Claude

Click the plus sign in the chat bar, or open Settings → Connectors. Connect Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, then sign in with Google and approve the permissions. Each is a separate toggle, so connect only what you need.

In ChatGPT

Open Settings → Apps and connect Gmail, Google Calendar and Drive. Connected apps need a paid plan, and on Business or Enterprise a workspace admin has to approve them before the connection will hold.

Set the folders up now

Six of the ten automations read from a Drive folder. Create these before you build anything: /Meetings/Transcripts, /Content/LongForm, /Content/Analytics, /Finance/Receipts. Empty folders are fine. The tasks will fill them.

Ground Rules

Six Things to Know First

These are the limits that trip people up on the first run. Read them once and every automation in this guide behaves the way you expect.

01 They run on a clock, not on an event

Neither platform fires when an email lands or a file appears. There are no event triggers. Everything here is a timer that looks back at what changed, which is why the cadences in this guide are chosen carefully.

02 Claude drafts email, it does not send it

Anthropic's own documentation is explicit: the Gmail connector creates drafts in your account but cannot send on your behalf. ChatGPT can send a single approved message on paid plans, though that send action is reported as unavailable in the UK and EU.

03 Cloud tasks cannot see your computer

A remote scheduled task has no access to a folder on your machine. Anything touching Downloads, Desktop or a local project has to run as a local task, which only fires while the app is open and the machine is awake.

04 Keep the approval mode tight at first

For anything that leaves your account or changes a file, have the task produce a draft and review it. Move to fully unattended only once you have watched it behave for a few weeks.

05 Watch the first three runs

Scheduled jobs drift. A folder gets renamed, an export changes shape, a connector needs reauthorising. Check the first three runs properly, then trust it and stop looking.

06 One at a time

Do not build all ten this week. Pick the one that costs you the most time, run it for a fortnight, then add the next. Ten half working automations are worse than one you rely on.

Automation 01: 7am Inbox Triage & Draft Replies

Claude & ChatGPT

Claude sorts your inbox by urgency and drafts replies to the easy ones overnight.

Trigger: Weekday mornings at 7:00, before you open your laptop.

It does: Reads everything since 5pm yesterday, sorts into high, medium and low, drafts the routine replies.

You get: A three line summary and a drafts folder that is already half your morning, done.

You'll need: Gmail or Microsoft 365, a paid plan on either tool.

Setup

Claude

Scheduled → New task → Setup manually

Runs: Weekdays, 7:00am

Needs: Gmail connector

ChatGPT

Scheduled → New task

Runs: Weekdays, 7:00am

Needs: Gmail app enabled in Apps

Note

Anthropic's Gmail connector creates drafts but cannot send. Every reply waits in your drafts for you to press send. ChatGPT can send a single approved message on paid plans, though that send action is reported as restricted in the UK and EU, so treat both as draft engines.

Paste this as the task prompt (Claude):

Search my inbox for every email received since 5pm yesterday.

Sort them into three buckets:
HIGH: needs a reply from me today
MEDIUM: needs a reply this week
LOW: FYI, newsletters, receipts, no reply needed

For anything in HIGH or MEDIUM with an obvious, low risk answer,
write the reply as a draft in my account. Short, direct, British
English, no filler, no "I hope this finds you well".

Do not draft anything that involves money, legal terms, a new
commitment or a refusal. List those under NEEDS YOU instead.

Finish with: counts per bucket, the three things that actually
matter today, and which drafts are waiting for me.

Paste this as the task prompt (ChatGPT):

Every weekday at 7am, check my Gmail for messages received
since 5pm the previous day.

Sort into HIGH, MEDIUM and LOW priority. For HIGH and MEDIUM
messages with a clear low risk answer, write a draft reply in
my voice: short, direct, British English.

Never draft anything involving money, legal terms, a new
commitment or a refusal. Flag those for me instead.

Reply with the bucket counts, my top three for today, and the
drafts you have written. Do not send anything without asking.

Automation 02: Pre-Meeting Dossier

Claude & ChatGPT

An hour before any call, Claude researches the person and sends you a one page brief.

Trigger: Runs hourly across your working day and looks ahead at the calendar.

It does: Pulls the attendee, their company, recent news and your past emails with them.

You get: One page: who they are, what changed, what to ask, what a good outcome looks like.

You'll need: Google Calendar, Gmail, web search on.

Setup

Claude

Scheduled → New task → Setup manually

Runs: Hourly, 8:00am to 6:00pm

Needs: Calendar, Gmail, web search

ChatGPT

Scheduled → New task

Runs: Hourly, working hours

Needs: Google Calendar app, Gmail app

Honest limit

Neither tool has event triggers, so nothing fires exactly sixty minutes before a call. Both run on a clock. Hourly is the tightest cadence ChatGPT allows, and looking 90 minutes ahead means you always get the brief before the meeting rather than during it.

Paste this as the task prompt (Claude):

Check my calendar for events starting in the next 90 minutes
that have at least one attendee outside my own domain.

If there are none, reply "Nothing upcoming" and stop.

For each one, research the external attendee and their company
and write me a single page brief:

OVERVIEW: who they are, role, how long they have been there
COMPANY: what it does, size, business model, funding if public
RECENT NEWS: anything from the last 90 days, with the source
OUR HISTORY: what my past emails with them say we agreed
TALKING POINTS: five, specific to them, not generic
QUESTIONS: three worth asking
GOOD OUTCOME: one line on what I want to leave with

Say when something is unverified rather than guessing.

Paste this as the task prompt (ChatGPT):

Look at my calendar for meetings starting in the next 90
minutes with an external attendee. If there are none, say
"Nothing upcoming" and stop.

For each meeting, search the web for the attendee and their
company, then give me a one page brief with these headings:
Overview, Company snapshot, Recent news, Past interactions,
Five talking points, Three questions, What a good outcome
looks like.

Keep it to one screen. Mark anything you could not verify.

Automation 03: Post-Meeting Recap to Task List

Claude & ChatGPT

Claude reads the call transcript and adds everything you agreed to straight into your tasks.

Trigger: Every weekday at 6pm, after the calls are done.

It does: Reads any new transcript, pulls the decisions, actions, owners and dates.

You get: A clean recap plus your own actions written into your task list.

You'll need: A notetaker saving transcripts, Google Drive or a synced folder, a task tool or a tasks file.

Setup

Claude

Scheduled → New task → Setup manually

Runs: Weekdays, 6:00pm

Needs: Drive connector, task connector if you have one

ChatGPT

Scheduled → New task

Runs: Weekdays, 6:00pm

Needs: Google Drive app, task app if connected

You'll need this first

Neither Claude nor ChatGPT sits in your calls. You need a notetaker that writes transcripts into a folder they can read, such as Drive. Task creation depends on which task connector you have available. If you have none, have it output the list and paste it in.

Paste this as the task prompt (Claude):

Check my Drive folder /Meetings/Transcripts for files added
in the last 24 hours. If there are none, stop.

For each transcript, produce:
DECISIONS: what was actually agreed, not what was discussed
ACTIONS: one line each, with owner and due date. If a date was
not said, propose one and mark it PROPOSED.
OPEN QUESTIONS: anything left unresolved
FOLLOW UP: any email that needs to go out, drafted

Then take only the actions owned by me and add them to my task
list with their due dates. Leave everyone else's in the recap.

Save the recap to /Meetings/Recaps as recap-[date]-[title].md

Do not invent an action that nobody committed to.

Paste this as the task prompt (ChatGPT):

Check my Google Drive folder Meetings/Transcripts for anything
added in the last 24 hours. If nothing is new, stop.

For each transcript give me: decisions agreed, action items with
an owner and a due date, open questions, and any follow up email
drafted and ready.

Then list my actions separately as a numbered task list I can
paste or add straight into my task app.

Never invent an action nobody committed to. If a due date was
not agreed, propose one and label it as proposed.

Automation 04: Friday 5pm Downloads & Desktop Cleanup

Claude & ChatGPT

Claude sorts your messy folders into place and deletes the junk, every Friday.

Trigger: Every Friday at 5pm, as you close the laptop.

It does: Scans Downloads and Desktop, dedupes, renames, sorts into real folders.

You get: A clean desktop and a report of what moved and what was quarantined.

You'll need: A local task, not a cloud one, Claude Code Desktop or the ChatGPT desktop app.

Setup

Claude

Claude Code Desktop → Routines → New routine → Local

Runs: Weekly, Friday 5:00pm

Needs: Local folder access

ChatGPT

Desktop app → Scheduled → New task, pointed at a local project

Runs: Weekly, Friday 5:00pm

Needs: Local project directory

Warning

This is the one that cannot run in the cloud. Scheduled tasks that run remotely have no access to folders on your computer. A local task only fires while the app is open and the machine is awake, so leave it running on a Friday. And never let it delete permanently. Quarantine, review, then delete yourself.

Paste this as the task prompt (Claude):

Tidy my Downloads and Desktop folders.

1. List everything, with size and date added.
2. Find exact duplicates. Keep the newest, move the rest to
_Review/Duplicates.
3. Rename anything with a meaningless name (IMG_9321, untitled,
data_v2) to something descriptive based on its contents.
4. Sort into: Invoices & Receipts, Contracts, Images,
Presentations, Other Documents. Create folders if missing.
5. Move obvious junk (.tmp, empty folders, half finished
downloads) to _Review/ToDelete. Do not delete anything
permanently.
6. Print a report: what moved where, what was renamed, what is
sitting in _Review waiting on me.

Never touch anything outside Downloads and Desktop.

Paste this as the task prompt (ChatGPT):

Every Friday at 5pm, tidy the folders in this project
directory.

Sort files into Invoices & Receipts, Contracts, Images,
Presentations and Other Documents. Rename anything with a
meaningless filename to something descriptive. Move exact
duplicates and obvious junk into a _Review folder rather than
deleting them.

Then give me a short report: how many files moved, what was
renamed, and what is waiting in _Review for me to confirm.

Automation 05: Monday 7:30am Founder Brief

Claude & ChatGPT

One message with your week ahead: meetings, DMs, cash, content due.

Trigger: Monday morning at 7:30, before anyone needs anything.

It does: Pulls the week's calendar, unanswered mail, your numbers and what is due.

You get: One message that replaces the first hour of Monday.

You'll need: Google Calendar, Gmail, a numbers sheet in Drive.

Setup

Claude

Scheduled → New task → Setup manually

Runs: Weekly, Monday 7:30am

Needs: Calendar, Gmail, Drive

ChatGPT

Scheduled → New task

Runs: Weekly, Monday morning

Needs: Calendar app, Gmail app, Drive app

Tip

The brief is only as good as what it can reach. If your numbers live in a bank app rather than a sheet, export a CSV into the same Drive folder once a week and point the prompt at it. Neither tool connects to a bank account directly.

Paste this as the task prompt (Claude):

Build my founder brief for the week ahead.

WEEK AHEAD: every meeting Monday to Sunday, day, time, who
with. Flag anything I have not prepared for.

NEEDS A REPLY: emails from the last seven days I have not
answered. Count them, then list the eight that matter, one line
each, with why.

CASH: read the latest figures in my Drive sheet
/Business/Numbers.xlsx and give me current balance, this
month's income and spend, and runway in weeks.

DUE THIS WEEK: anything with a deadline in the next seven days.

CONTENT: what I said I would publish this week and what is
still unmade.

Then, FOCUS: the three things that would make this week a win.
Be specific and pick the boring, high leverage ones. If the
numbers or the calendar look off, say so.

Paste this as the task prompt (ChatGPT):

Every Monday at 7:30am, write my week at a glance.

Include: every meeting this week with day and time; how many
emails are waiting on a reply plus the eight that matter most;
the latest numbers from my Drive spreadsheet with runway; tasks
and deadlines falling this week; and the content I planned to
publish.

Finish with the three things to focus on this week and one line
on why each one moves the needle. Keep the whole thing under a
screen and a half.

Automation 06: Monthly Expense Organiser

Claude & ChatGPT

Claude pulls receipts from your inbox, sorts by category, ready for your accountant.

Trigger: The 1st of every month, for the month just gone.

It does: Finds every receipt and invoice, extracts the detail, groups and totals it.

You get: A clean spreadsheet, categorised, with the gaps flagged.

You'll need: Gmail, Google Drive, card statement CSV in a folder.

Setup

Claude

Scheduled → New task → Setup manually

Runs: Monthly, 1st at 9:00am

Needs: Gmail, Drive

ChatGPT

Scheduled → New task

Runs: Monthly, 1st of the month

Needs: Gmail app, Drive app

Note

Card and bank transactions are not reachable from either tool. Export the month's statement as a CSV into the same folder and the prompt above will reconcile against it. Attachment contents in Gmail are limited too, so keep PDF receipts saved into Drive where they can be read properly.

Paste this as the task prompt (Claude):

Collect every business expense from last month.

Search my inbox for receipts, invoices, order confirmations and
subscription renewals dated last month. Also check my Drive
folder /Finance/Receipts and any statement CSV in
/Finance/Statements.

For each one extract: date, vendor, description, category,
amount, currency, VAT if shown, and where you found it.
Categories: Software, Advertising, Contractors, Equipment,
Travel, Subscriptions, Office, Other.

Build a spreadsheet in /Finance/Reports named
expenses-[month]-[year].xlsx, one row per expense, plus a
summary sheet totalling by category and by vendor.

Finish with total spend, the number of expenses, and a MISSING
list where an amount, VAT or vendor is unclear. Never guess a
figure. Flag it instead.

Paste this as the task prompt (ChatGPT):

On the 1st of each month, find every receipt, invoice and
subscription renewal from last month in my Gmail and my Drive
receipts folder.

Pull out date, vendor, description, category, amount, currency
and VAT for each one. Categorise them, total them by category
and by vendor, and give me the result as a table I can export.

List separately anything where a figure is unclear or a receipt
looks missing. Never estimate an amount.

Automation 07: Daily 6pm Build Log to Content Bank

Claude & ChatGPT

Ask what you worked on today, Claude turns it into tomorrow's content hooks.

Trigger: Every day at 6pm, one question lands.

It does: Takes your two line answer and mines it for the actual story.

You get: Two or three hooks saved to your content bank, ready for tomorrow.

You'll need: Notifications on, a content bank doc in Drive.

Setup

Claude

Scheduled → New task → Setup manually

Runs: Daily, 6:00pm

Needs: Drive connector

ChatGPT

Scheduled → New task

Runs: Daily, 6:00pm

Needs: Drive app optional

How this one actually works

A scheduled run cannot pause and wait for you. What happens is the 6pm run asks the question and notifies you, then you reply in that same thread and the hooks come back straight away. Two minutes of typing on your phone, and tomorrow's content is written.

Paste this as the task prompt (Claude):

Ask me one question and stop: "What did you work on today?"

When I reply, turn my answer into two or three short form
content hooks for tomorrow.

Rules for the hooks:
- Lead with the specific thing that happened, not the lesson
- A number, a mistake or a result in the first line
- Written how I talk, not how a brand talks
- No "in today's video", no "let's dive in"
- Each one: the hook line, then three bullets for the body

Then append them to /Content/content-bank.md under today's date,
with a one word tag for the format (talking head, screen record,
story).

If my answer is thin, ask one follow up question before writing.

Paste this as the task prompt (ChatGPT):

Every day at 6pm, message me with one question: "What did you
work on today?"

When I answer, turn it into two or three content hooks for
tomorrow. Lead each one with the specific thing that happened,
not the lesson. Include a number, a mistake or a result in the
first line. Write it how I talk. No "in today's video".

Give me the hook line plus three body bullets for each, then
save them to my content bank file with today's date.

Automation 08: Weekly Content Repurposing

Claude & ChatGPT

One long video in, a week of shorts and carousel copy out, in your voice.

Trigger: Every Monday, against whatever you dropped in the folder.

It does: Breaks the long form into core ideas, then rewrites each one for its platform.

You get: Five shorts, a carousel, a post, a short and a newsletter snippet.

You'll need: A transcript in Drive, a voice sample doc.

Setup

Claude

Scheduled → New task → Setup manually

Runs: Weekly, Monday 8:00am

Needs: Drive connector

ChatGPT

Scheduled → New task

Runs: Weekly, Monday morning

Needs: Drive app

Note

A scheduled run cannot accept a file upload at the moment it fires. Keep the transcript in a connected Drive folder and the task will pick up whatever is newest. The voice sample matters more than the prompt. Paste in three of your own captions and the output stops sounding like a brand.

Paste this as the task prompt (Claude):

Read the newest transcript in /Content/LongForm and my voice
sample in /Content/voice.md.

Pull out the core ideas worth their own piece of content, then
write all of the following in my voice:

5 x short form scripts, 30 to 45 seconds. Hook, body, last
line. One idea each, never a recap of the whole video.
1 x Instagram carousel, 7 slides, under 15 words per slide
1 x LinkedIn post, 150 to 200 words, no hashtags
1 x YouTube Short script, tighter than the others
1 x newsletter snippet, 120 words, ending on one action

British English, no em dashes, no "in this video", no filler.
If an idea is too thin to carry its own video, say so rather
than padding it out.

Save it all to /Content/Repurposed/week-[date].md

Paste this as the task prompt (ChatGPT):

Read the newest transcript in my Drive folder Content/LongForm
and the voice sample saved alongside it.

Break it into the core ideas, then write in my voice: five short
form scripts of 30 to 45 seconds, one seven slide Instagram
carousel, one LinkedIn post of 150 to 200 words, one YouTube
Short script and one 120 word newsletter snippet.

British English. No hashtags, no "in this video", no filler.
Tell me if an idea is too thin to carry its own piece rather
than padding it.

Automation 09: Brand Deal & DM Scanner

Claude & ChatGPT

Claude checks every brand email against your playbook and flags the DMs worth replying to.

Trigger: Twice a day, midday and end of day.

It does: Scores every inbound against your rates, red lines and deliverables.

You get: Worth replying, worth negotiating, or pass. With the reply already drafted.

You'll need: Gmail, a playbook doc with your rates, DMs forwarded to that inbox.

Setup

Claude

Scheduled → New task → Setup manually

Runs: Daily, 12:00pm and 5:00pm

Needs: Gmail, Drive

ChatGPT

Scheduled → New task

Runs: Twice daily

Needs: Gmail app, Drive app

Honest limit

There is no official connector to Instagram or TikTok DMs, so nothing can read them directly. The workaround that actually holds: set your DM auto reply to point brands at your email, and forward the serious ones in yourself. The playbook doc is what makes this work. Without it, it is just a summariser.

Paste this as the task prompt (Claude):

Read my playbook at /Business/brand-playbook.md first. It has
my rate floor, usage terms, exclusivity limits and red lines.

Then search my inbox since the last run for any brand,
sponsorship, gifting or partnership approach, including
messages I have forwarded in from social DMs.

Score each against the playbook:
- Fee at or above my floor, or no fee named at all
- Usage rights and exclusivity, and for how long
- Deliverables versus the fee, and the turnaround
- Anything sitting on my red lines list

Classify each as WORTH REPLYING, NEGOTIATE or PASS with one
line of reasoning, then draft a reply for the first two. Warm,
brief, and always ask for the fee if none was named.

Never accept, decline or commit on my behalf.

Paste this as the task prompt (ChatGPT):

Read my brand playbook in Drive, then check my inbox since the
last run for brand, sponsorship, gifting or partnership emails,
including forwarded DMs.

Score each against the playbook: fee versus my floor, usage
rights, exclusivity, deliverables, turnaround, and any red
lines. Classify as worth replying, negotiate, or pass, with one
line of reasoning.

Draft a short reply for the first two. Always ask for the fee if
none was named. Do not commit to anything on my behalf.

Automation 10: Weekly Content Performance Digest

Claude & ChatGPT

Every Sunday: what worked, what flopped, what to make more of.

Trigger: Every Sunday evening, on the week just finished.

It does: Compares this week's numbers to the last four, in plain English.

You get: What worked, what flopped, what to make more of, and three ideas.

You'll need: Analytics exports in Drive, four weeks of history to compare.

Setup

Claude

Scheduled → New task → Setup manually

Runs: Weekly, Sunday 6:00pm

Needs: Drive connector

ChatGPT

Scheduled → New task

Runs: Weekly, Sunday evening

Needs: Drive app

Note

There is no official Instagram, TikTok or YouTube analytics connector on either platform. Export the CSV from each app's native analytics into one Drive folder each Sunday. It takes two minutes and it is the only part of this you do by hand.

Paste this as the task prompt (Claude):

Read every export in /Content/Analytics, including the previous
four weeks, not just this one.

Write my weekly digest:

WHAT WORKED: the three best performing posts. For each: the
numbers, and your best read on why it worked. Watch time and
saves matter more than views, so weight them that way.

WHAT FLOPPED: the two weakest. Same treatment. Be blunt.

PATTERNS: anything true across four weeks rather than one good
post. Format, length, hook style, posting time.

MAKE MORE OF: three specific things, not "post consistently".

NEXT WEEK: three content ideas built on what is working, with
a hook line for each.

Plain English, no marketing language. If a week is too small a
sample to conclude anything, say that instead of inventing a
trend.

Paste this as the task prompt (ChatGPT):

Every Sunday, read the analytics exports in my Drive folder,
including the last four weeks.

Tell me: the three posts that worked and why, the two that
flopped and why, any pattern that holds across four weeks
rather than one post, three specific things to make more of,
and three ideas for next week with a hook line each.

Weight watch time and saves above views. Plain English. If the
sample is too small to draw a conclusion, say so.

Ten Automations. Start With One.

Every one of these is a saved prompt and a cadence. That is the whole trick. The tools are already on your account, the connectors take two minutes, and the prompts are written out in full on the pages you just read.

  • Pick one: The one that costs you the most time this week. Not the most exciting one.
  • Run it manually first: Paste the prompt into a normal chat and check the output before you schedule anything.
  • Then schedule it: Same prompt, saved as a task, on the cadence in this guide.
  • Watch three runs: Then leave it alone and add the next one.
