Chapter 01: The Opportunity

ScrapeGraphAI is a real, open-source project: an AI-powered web scraping library with an MIT licence and close to 29,500 stars on GitHub. It lets a language model like Claude read the instruction "find me every gym owner in Manchester with a public email" and actually go and do it, searching the web, pulling the right fields off each page, and handing back structured data instead of a pile of raw HTML.

ScrapeGraphAI/Scrapegraph-ai

Tools like Apify typically charge per run: every new company you scrape costs you again. ScrapeGraphAI's hosted API, which is the part that actually plugs into Claude, works on a credit system with a free tier instead. For a modest daily lead list, most people won't touch a paid tier at all.

Note: On "Completely Free"

The open-source library is genuinely free (MIT licence, no cost to run). But the piece that connects to Claude is ScrapeGraphAI's hosted API, and that runs on a free tier plus paid credits beyond it, not unlimited scraping forever. Chapter 02 shows you exactly what the free tier includes.

What You're Actually Building

Open-source library

The code in the GitHub repo. You run it yourself, bring your own LLM key, and manage the browser and proxies. Free, but more setup.

Hosted API (what plugs into Claude)

A managed service with a free tier of credits, then pay-as-you-go. Nothing to run yourself, this is what Chapter 03 connects.

  • Claude searches the web or a specific directory on your instruction
  • It pulls out business names, websites and public contact details
  • It hands you a clean, structured list you can turn into a sheet
  • You can schedule the same search to run again each morning

Chapter 02: Get Your ScrapeGraphAI Account

About two minutes, most of it is a sign-up form.

1. Go to scrapegraphai.com and sign up

Use the "Sign up" button in the top right. Signing in with Google is the fastest option and it's also what makes Chapter 03's connector step easiest.

2. Open your dashboard

Once you're signed in, you land on the ScrapeGraphAI dashboard. This is where your API key lives, if you'd rather authenticate with a key than with Google sign-in.

3. Note your starting credits

New accounts start with a free tier of credits, enough to complete this whole guide and run a handful of extra searches afterwards. You can check your balance any time from the dashboard.

Tip: If you plan to run this daily (Chapter 06), keep an eye on your credit balance for the first week so you know whether the free tier covers your habit.

4. Keep the tab open

You'll come back to this dashboard in the next chapter, either to copy your API key or to confirm the Google account you'll sign in with.

How the credits work

Each search, extract or crawl through ScrapeGraphAI uses a small number of credits depending on how much it has to fetch and read. The free tier is enough for testing this guide and running occasional lead lists by hand. If you move to the daily automation in Chapter 06, you'll likely want to check pricing on the dashboard once your free credits run low.

Chapter 03: Connect It to Claude

Warning: Not "Paste the Repo Link"

Copying the GitHub link and pasting it into a Claude chat doesn't connect anything. That link is the source code for the open-source library. What actually gives Claude scraping tools is adding ScrapeGraphAI as a custom connector, using the steps below.

1. Open Claude and go to Connectors

In Claude, open Settings, then Customize, then Connectors.

2. Add a custom connector

Click the "+" next to Connectors, then choose "Add custom connector".

3. Paste the ScrapeGraphAI server URL

Give it a name, e.g. "ScrapeGraphAI", and paste this as the remote MCP server URL:

https://mcp.scrapegraphai.com/mcp

4. Click Add, then Connect

Sign in with the Google account tied to your ScrapeGraphAI account, or paste your API key if you'd rather authenticate that way.

5. Switch it on for your chat

Open a new conversation, open the tools menu, and turn ScrapeGraphAI on for that chat. Connectors aren't enabled everywhere by default.

Note

Custom connectors work in Claude on the web, in Claude Desktop, and in Cowork, on free and paid plans alike. Free plans are limited to one custom connector at a time.

How to know it worked

Ask Claude to check your ScrapeGraphAI credit balance. A real number back means the connector is live end to end. An error or a generic "I can't check that" means the connection or the toggle needs another look.

Chapter 04: Run Your First Lead Search

With the connector switched on, Claude can search the web and pull structured data through ScrapeGraphAI's tools. Describe exactly who you're looking for and what you want back, in plain English.

Example prompt:

Find 20 gym owners in Manchester with a public website and a
contact email. For each one, give me: business name, website,
contact email, and city. Put it in a table.

Tip

Be specific about the niche, the location, and exactly what "found" should include. "Gym owners in Manchester with a public email" beats "fitness businesses" every time. A narrow prompt returns a usable list; a vague one returns noise you'll have to clean up by hand.

Fields you can ask for: Business name, Website, Public email, Phone number, LinkedIn profile, City / address, Employee count

Follow-up prompt:

Drop anything without a public email. For the rest, also find
their Instagram handle if they have one.

Chapter 05: Turn Results Into a Clean Sheet

A table in the chat is useful, but a sheet you can sort, filter and hand to someone else is better. You don't need a separate tool for this, just ask.

Export prompt:

Turn this into an Excel file with the same columns, and sort it
alphabetically by business name.

Two Ways to Get Your Sheet

Download a spreadsheet

Ask Claude to build the list as an Excel file. It generates a downloadable .xlsx with the same columns, ready to open in Excel, Sheets, or Numbers.

Push straight to Google Sheets

If you also have a Google Sheets or Drive connector enabled, ask Claude to add the results directly to an existing sheet instead of a download.

Note

Pushing straight into Google Sheets needs that connector added and switched on separately, it isn't part of the ScrapeGraphAI connection. Add it the same way as Chapter 03: Settings → Customize → Connectors.

Chapter 06: Automate It

Warning: This Doesn't Happen by Itself

Asking Claude to find leads once doesn't make it repeat that search every morning on its own. To get a daily refill, you save the prompt as a scheduled task in Claude Cowork.

1. Open Cowork

Cowork is Anthropic's agentic workspace for handing off recurring, multi-step work, and it's where scheduled tasks live.

2. Describe the recurring job once

Something like: "Every morning, find any new gym owners in Manchester that weren't in yesterday's list, and add them to the sheet."

3. Set the cadence to daily

Choose how often it runs. Cowork saves your connectors, including ScrapeGraphAI, along with the task, so it doesn't need re-authorising each morning.

4. Confirm and let it run

Each run is its own Cowork session. Come back whenever you like to review the fresh leads it's added.

Note

Scheduled tasks in Cowork are available on paid Claude plans (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise), not the free plan. Everything before this chapter works on a free account; this last step is the one that needs an upgrade.

Morning Isn't the Only Option

Cowork's scheduled tasks aren't locked to daily runs. Pick hourly if you're testing a hot niche, weekdays-only to skip weekends when nobody's checking, or weekly if a fresh batch every Monday is plenty. Set the cadence to whatever actually matches how often you follow up on leads.

Chapter 07: Prompts & Troubleshooting

Prompts to Get You Started

Local business owners

Find 15 independent coffee shop owners in Leeds with a public email address.

Find by role

Find marketing managers at UK ecommerce brands with under 50 staff, with a public LinkedIn profile.

Refresh a list

Compare this to yesterday's list and only add companies that weren't already on it.

Export

Turn the results into a spreadsheet with these columns: name, company, email, website.

Narrow a big list

From this list, keep only the businesses with fewer than 10 employees and a live Instagram account.

If Something Doesn't Work

Connector won't authenticate

Check you're signing in with the same Google account, or the same API key, that's tied to your ScrapeGraphAI account.

Claude says it can't browse the web

Make sure ScrapeGraphAI is switched on for that specific chat in the tools menu. Connectors aren't on in every conversation by default.

Credits running low

Check your balance from the ScrapeGraphAI dashboard. Daily scheduled runs use credits faster than occasional one-off searches.

Before You Schedule It Daily

Run the search manually two or three times first. Confirm the fields come back clean and the niche is narrow enough, then hand the exact same prompt to Cowork. It's much easier to fix a prompt once than to untangle a week of scheduled runs built on a vague one.
