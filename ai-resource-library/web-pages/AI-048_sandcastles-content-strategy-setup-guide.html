Chapter 01: The Problem With Guessing What Works

Most creators spend hours scrolling through their niche, trying to reverse-engineer what's working. They watch videos, make notes, and try to spot patterns. Then they guess. And most of the time, they guess wrong.

The problem isn't effort. It's that manual research can't keep pace with the volume of data that actually explains content performance. You can watch twenty videos and miss the pattern that's hiding across two hundred. The real signal is buried in aggregate data: which hook types are driving views across an entire channel, which formats consistently outperform, and which topics are resonating in your niche right now, not six months ago.

The Core Problem

Content creation without data becomes reactive. You copy what competitors just did, post on schedule because it's Tuesday, and wonder why nothing compounds. Systematic research replaces guesswork with repeatable decisions.

Chapter 02: How Sandcastles Works

Sandcastles is a content research and scriptwriting platform built specifically for short-form video. It analyses millions of videos across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube to surface the top performing content in any niche, then explains in detail why each video worked.

The Four Core Features

01
Channel Tracking and Watchlists

Add any creator or channel across all three platforms into a watchlist. Sandcastles monitors their output continuously and ranks videos by outlier score, so you always see what's performing, not just what's recent.

02
Outlier Score Ranking

Every video gets an outlier score: a measure of how much it over-performed relative to the channel's baseline. Sort by outlier score over any timeframe (14 days, 40 days, 90 days) to find the true standouts, not just the most viewed.

03
Full Transcript and Deep Analysis

Every outlier video comes with a full transcript plus a detailed breakdown of its hook type, storytelling structure, format, and style. This is what tells you why a video worked, not just that it did.

04
Hook Engine and Script Tools

Once you know what's working, Sandcastles generates hook variations using proven templates that have driven millions of views. These feed directly into its scriptwriting tools or export into Claude for further development.

The Claude Connection

Sandcastles exports analysis directly into Claude via its MCP connector. This means Claude gets structured data it can reason over, not just a paste of text. It can compare patterns, identify what's consistent across outliers, and build a content strategy from the signal in that data.

Chapter 03: Setting Up the Sandcastles Connector

The Sandcastles connector is an MCP integration that creates a live data bridge between Sandcastles and Claude. Once connected, Claude can pull channel data, outlier rankings, transcripts, and analysis directly into any conversation.

01 Create your Sandcastles account. Go to sandcastles.ai and sign up. The 7-day free trial gives full access to all features including the Claude connector. Plans start at $39 per month billed annually after the trial.

02 Build your first watchlist. Inside Sandcastles, add the channels you want to analyse. These can be competitors, top creators in your niche, or your own channel. The platform tracks Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube in one place.

03 Connect Sandcastles to Claude. In Claude, go to Settings, then Connectors. Search for Sandcastles or add it as a custom connector using the MCP server URL from your Sandcastles account settings. Authorise via OAuth when prompted.

04 Enable the connector in your conversation. Click the + button at the bottom of any Claude chat, select Connectors, and toggle Sandcastles on. Claude now has live access to your watchlist data and outlier analysis.

05 Run your first analysis. Paste a channel link or name a creator from your watchlist. Claude pulls their top performing videos, scores them by outlier performance, and begins the analysis automatically.

Connector Availability

The Sandcastles MCP connector works on Claude's Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans. It is accessible via claude.ai, Claude Desktop, and the mobile apps once connected through your account settings.

Chapter 05: Turning Insights Into a Content Plan

The analysis is only useful if it drives action. Once Claude has run the channel recap, the next step is turning those findings into a concrete content plan: specific video ideas, hook frameworks, and a production order based on what the data says will work.

The Three-Step Content Workflow

01 Run the channel recap. Pull the top outliers for your channel or a competitor's channel. Set the timeframe to whatever gives you a meaningful sample (40 days is a good default for most active channels).

02 Extract the repeating patterns. Ask Claude to identify what's consistent across the top performers. Look for hook type, opening format, topic category, and video length. The pattern that appears across multiple outliers is the signal worth building on.

03 Generate your video ideas from the signal. Give Claude your niche, your voice, and the patterns it found. Ask it to generate five to ten video ideas using the hook types and formats that are proven to outperform. These ideas are not guesses. They are built on what is already working in your space.

Run It On Your Own Channel When
  • Planning your next content batch
  • A video outperforms unexpectedly
  • Growth has stalled or plateaued
  • You want to find what to double down on
Run It On Competitors When
  • Entering or researching a new niche
  • Looking for angles they haven't covered
  • Finding what's working at larger scale
  • Building a benchmark for your own strategy

This workflow replaces hours of manual research with a structured, data-backed process you can repeat every week. Instead of guessing what content works, you pull the answer straight from the data.
