HARVARD

The deepest one on this list: search algorithms, classification, optimisation, reinforcement learning and machine learning, built into your own Python programs through hands-on projects. Best pick if you want to understand the mechanics.

A free online course for anyone wanting to learn what AI is, what's possible (and not) with it, and how it affects our lives: no complicated maths or programming required. The classic non-technical AI literacy course.

Free LinkedIn certificate
elementsofai.com ↗
