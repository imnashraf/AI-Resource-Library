Figma + Claude Setup Guide

Max Johnson @maxjohnscn

Turn Any Concept Into a Visual Diagram Without Touching Figma.

This guide gets you connected in about 30 seconds. Once the Figma integration is active, Claude can build proper visual diagrams directly inside Figma from a single prompt, no design experience needed.

What you will have when this is done:

  • The Figma connector active inside Claude
  • Claude building diagrams directly into Figma from a prompt
  • A reusable workflow for mapping any concept, process, or system visually

Before you start. You need two things already in place:

  • A Claude account, free plan works fine
  • A Figma account, free plan works fine

NOTE: You do not need to have anything open in Figma before you start. Claude will create a new file automatically when it builds the diagram.

Step 01, Connect the Figma Integration, Takes about 30 seconds

Go to Claude and click on the integrations or tools section. Find the Figma connector in the list and click connect. You will be asked to sign into your Figma account and approve access. That is the entire setup.

TIP: The Figma connector appears under Tools or Integrations depending on which version of Claude you are using. If you cannot find it, go to claude.ai/settings and look under Integrations.

Step 02, Ask Claude to Create a Diagram, One prompt is all it takes

With the Figma connector active, type your prompt directly into Claude. You do not need any special syntax or commands. Just describe what you want mapped out and Claude handles the rest.

$ Claude, example prompts to try Create a diagram of my user onboarding flow Map out the architecture of a SaaS subscription billing system Build a visual diagram of the decision making process for a job application Create a flowchart showing how a webhook triggers events in my app

TIP: Be specific about what you are mapping. The more context you give Claude about the process or system, the more accurate and detailed the diagram will be.

NOTE: Claude builds the diagram directly inside Figma and gives you a link to open it. The file will appear in your Figma drafts automatically.

Step 03, Open and Refine in Figma, Iterate from inside Claude

Click the Figma link Claude gives you. The diagram opens in your browser inside Figma. If you want to change anything, add a step, restructure a section, change the layout, come back to Claude and ask it to update. You do not need to edit Figma manually.

$ Claude, refining your diagram Add a step between onboarding and first login that shows email verification Restructure this as a swimlane diagram with three lanes Add a decision point at the payment step with two outcomes

TIP: Claude can make targeted edits to an existing diagram in the same conversation. You do not need to rebuild from scratch, just describe the change and it updates the Figma file directly.

What to Use It For, Anything You Need to Think Through Visually

The Figma connector works for any kind of diagram:

  • User flows and onboarding journeys
  • System and app architecture
  • Business process maps
  • Decision trees
  • Org charts
  • API and webhook flows
  • Content or marketing funnels

TIP: The fastest way to use this is to describe the thing you are trying to understand out loud, paste it into Claude, and ask it to map it. You do not need to structure your thinking first, that is the point of the diagram.

Both Claude and Figma are free to get started. No paid plan needed for this workflow.
