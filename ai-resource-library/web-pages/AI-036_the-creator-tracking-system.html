Chapter 01: The Content Pillar System

Most creators track content by volume: how many posts went out, how often, which days. The problem is that tells you how busy you were, not why something worked.

A pillar system organises your content into distinct categories before you post it. Every piece belongs to a pillar. Every pillar has a job. When you review your performance at the end of the month, you're not just looking at numbers: you're looking at which categories are driving which outcomes.

That distinction is the difference between being a content creator and being a content operator.

The core idea: Assign every piece of content to a pillar before it goes out. The pillar defines the job. The job defines the metric. The metric defines whether it's working: and what to do more of.

Pillar 01
Tutorial / Tool
Job: Reach & Growth
Highest-volume, widest-reach. You're showing people how to do something specific. These posts travel because they're useful to people who've never heard of you.
Views Saves New Followers
Pillar 02
Concept / Insight
Job: Authority & Retention
Teach the thinking behind the tools. Not how to use something but why it matters, what's changing, what most people are missing.
Comments Shares Watch Time
Pillar 03
Social Proof / Results
Job: Conversion
These are the posts that turn followers into community members or buyers. Real outcomes. You don't need many: you need them to be specific.
Link Clicks Sign-Ups DMs
Pillar 04
Founder / Behind the Scenes
Job: Trust & Relatability
Reminds people there's a real person doing this. This pillar makes the other three feel credible because it shows the work behind them.
Engagement Rate Saves Story Replies

Chapter 02: The Five-Tab Spreadsheet

The tracking sheet has five tabs. Each has a specific job. Together they give you everything you need to run your content like a system rather than a series of individual posts.

Tab 1
Monthly Content Log

Every piece of content entered here after posting. Columns: Date, Platform, Pillar, Content Title, Format (short-form / long-form / static), Views, Reach, Saves, Comments, Shares, Follows, Link Clicks. One row per post.

Tab 2
Monthly Pillar Breakdown

Auto-calculated from Tab 1. Shows volume per pillar, average views per pillar, total saves, and pillar share of total reach. This is the tab you look at when deciding what to make more of.

Tab 3
Platform Comparison

Same content logged across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts. See where each pillar performs best by platform.

Tab 4
Growth Tracker

Monthly follower counts across platforms, net new followers per month, and a running total. One row per month.

Tab 5
90-Day Forward Plan

Content planned but not yet posted. Pillar assigned, platform assigned, rough title. Not a rigid calendar: a thinking tool.

Tip: Tab 2 is the most important tab for decision-making. Once a month, look at which pillar is underperforming and ask whether it's a volume problem or a quality problem before changing anything.

Chapter 03: Agent Mode Prompts: Build & Analyse

These are the prompts worth actually using inside Agent Mode in Excel. Each one is built around a specific job to be done. Paste your data in, run the prompt, get something useful back.

Prompt 1: Build the Full Tracking System

Use this when setting up for the first time. Give Agent Mode a blank workbook and paste this in.

Prompt 1
Build me a social media content tracking system with five tabs. Tab 1 is a Monthly Content Log with columns for Date, Platform, Pillar, Content Title, Format, Views, Reach, Saves, Comments, Shares, New Followers, and Link Clicks. Tab 2 is a Pillar Breakdown that automatically calculates total posts, average views, total saves, and percentage of total reach for each of my four pillars: Tutorial, Concept, Social Proof, and Founder. Tab 3 is a Platform Comparison that shows average views and saves by platform per pillar. Tab 4 is a Growth Tracker with monthly follower counts and net new followers. Tab 5 is a Forward Plan with columns for Planned Date, Platform, Pillar, and Content Title. Apply conditional formatting to highlight any pillar producing below- average views. Make it clean and easy to use.

Prompt 2: Monthly Performance Analysis

Paste in a month of data from Tab 1 and run this.

Prompt 2
Analyse this content performance data. Tell me which pillar is driving the most reach, which is driving the most saves, and which is driving the most new followers. Identify if any pillar is underperforming. Flag any individual posts that significantly outperformed the average and suggest what they might have in common. Give me three specific things I should do differently next month based on this data.

Chapter 04: Agent Mode Prompts: Plan & Grow

Prompt 3: Platform Breakdown

Prompt 3
Using this data, compare performance across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts. For each platform, tell me which content pillar is performing best and which is performing worst. Identify if the same content is resonating differently across platforms and explain why. Create a summary table showing average views, saves, and follower growth by platform and by pillar.

Prompt 4: Growth Forecasting

Use with at least two to three months of data in the Growth Tracker tab.

Prompt 4
Based on my follower growth data across the last [X] months, project how long it would take to reach [target follower count] if current trends continue. Tell me what growth rate I'd need to hit that target in half the time. Based on which content pillars are currently driving the most new followers, suggest how I should shift my content mix to increase that rate. Build a simple chart showing the current trajectory and the accelerated one.

Prompt 5: Content Mix Planning

Prompt 5
I want to plan my content for the next month. I post [X] times per week across [platforms]. Based on my historical performance data, suggest a content mix by pillar that would maximise reach while maintaining enough Concept and Founder content to protect engagement rate. Give me a recommended split as a percentage, tell me why, and build a 30-day content calendar with placeholder titles organised by pillar.

Prompt 6: Identify the Pattern in Your Best Posts

Prompt 6
Filter my content log to the top 10 posts by views and the top 10 by saves. Identify patterns across both lists: pillar, platform, format, day of the week, content topic. Tell me what the top posts have in common that the average posts don't. Flag any posts that appear in both lists these are my highest-value content types.

Chapter 05: The Quarterly Health Check

At the end of every quarter, run a full audit of your pillar system. This prompt looks at three months at once and tells you exactly where the strategy is working and where it needs changing.

Prompt 7: Pillar Health Check

Prompt 7
Run a quarterly health check on my content pillars using the last three months of data. For each pillar, tell me: is volume appropriate relative to its job, is it hitting its primary KPI (reach for Tutorial, engagement for Concept, conversions for Social Proof, trust signals for Founder), and is it trending up or down over the three months. Summarise the overall health of the content strategy in three sentences and give me two priorities for next quarter.
Pillar Primary Signal Warning Sign Action if Underperforming
Tutorial / Tool Views + Saves Reach is flat Topic or hook is the problem: not the format
Concept / Insight Comments + Shares Watch time drops Opening isn't earning attention fast enough
Social Proof / Results Link Clicks + DMs Low conversion Proof isn't specific enough
Founder / BTS Engagement Rate + Story Replies Engagement is flat Less polish, more real

Tutorial / Tool: Primary signal: Views and Saves. If reach is flat, the topic or hook is the problem: not the format.

Concept / Insight: Primary signal: Comments and Shares. If watch time drops, the opening isn't earning attention fast enough.

Social Proof / Results: Primary signal: Link Clicks and DMs. If these aren't converting, the proof isn't specific enough.

Founder / BTS: Primary signal: Engagement Rate and Story Replies. Lower reach is normal: high engagement is the goal.

The core principle: You're not just a content creator. You're a content operator. The difference is that operators use data to make decisions before they make content: not after.

Build the System. Run the Prompts.

Set up your five-tab tracker, assign every post to a pillar from day one, and run these Agent Mode prompts at the end of each month. Within 90 days you'll know exactly what to make more of: and exactly what to stop.
